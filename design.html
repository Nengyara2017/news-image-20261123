Ukraine · Libya · War at Sea

Zelenskyy: Ukraine Sank Russian Warship in Libya

Seven Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base at dawn on Sunday, 2,400 km from the front line, and destroyed a Ka-27 helicopter aboard. A cargo ship used to carry weapons was also hit. At least 10 people were wounded.

UN climate talks ended on Sunday, two days late, with no agreement on fossil fuels or money. Nearly 200 countries took part.

PM Andy Burnham told MPs on Monday the deal struck with the EU on Friday is worth £17bn, scraps most food-export checks and opens a youth scheme for 100,000 young people.
